# Brainrot Academy v0.7.1 — Rebrand Build

- Official app name changed from **Brainrot Education** to **Brainrot Academy**.
- “ARE YOU SMARTER THAN A BRAINROT?” remains the internal game-show challenge hook.
- Professor Brainrot remains the primary mascot and academy personality.
- Updated app title, PWA manifest, package identity, report branding, startup/home wordmark, and offline cache namespace.
- Renamed the primary SVG wordmark to `brainrot-academy-logo.svg` and changed the visible subtitle to **ACADEMY**.
- Added non-destructive preference migration: existing `brainrotMathPrefs` and `brainrotEducationPrefs` data is inherited, while future saves use `brainrotAcademyPrefs`.
- Gameplay, curriculum, audio, characters, and progress behavior are otherwise unchanged from v0.7.
