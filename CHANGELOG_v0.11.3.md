# Brainrot Academy v0.11.3 — Glorp Joke Answers

This update focuses on **Glorp's voice and comedy**.

## Highlights
- Wrong-answer reactions now use **subject-specific absurd joke lines**.
- Math wrong answers include clearly silly lines like **"7 × 7 = cheeseburger."** and **"6 × 5 = ice cream."**
- Added more joke-answer banks for spelling, grammar, science, geography, reading, vocabulary, time, calendar, and general knowledge.
- Professor feedback now explicitly clarifies that Glorp's absurd answers are **jokes**, reinforcing the real educational answer.

## Notes
- No scoring or curriculum logic changed.
- Correct answers and explanations still drive learning.
- The joke lines are deliberately absurd so children will not confuse them with real instruction.
